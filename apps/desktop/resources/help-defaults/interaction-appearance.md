# Interaction Appearance And Completion

Open **Settings → Interaction appearance**, or click the sliders button on the Task journey. These preferences apply across Chat and Build and are saved on this device.

## Choose An Interaction Style

| Style | Presentation |
| --- | --- |
| Calm | Quiet presentation without decorative task movement or completion sound. |
| Balanced | A gentle avatar gesture and subtle result highlight on success. |
| Playful | A cheerful avatar gesture and brief sparkle on success. |

1. Select **Calm**, **Balanced**, or **Playful**.
2. Set **Completion animations** as desired.
3. Optionally enable **Soft completion chime**. It defaults to off and is suppressed in Calm.
4. Close the panel. The selections are retained.

Your operating system's reduced-motion preference takes precedence over completion animation settings. Status and results remain visible with effects off.

## When Completion Effects Appear

Effects follow an observed active-to-successful transition with an answer. They are brief and do not delay the result. They do not replay when opening old history, and they do not celebrate failures, stopped turns, or guidance requests awaiting your choice.

A success effect marks the recorded turn completing; it does not independently verify facts, tests, or code changes. Use the answer and Task journey evidence to assess the result.

## Other Appearance Controls

Interaction style controls task effects. Chat background selection and agent/avatar configuration remain separate controls. Use the chat **Appearance** controls for the background, and **Settings → Agents** for agent identity and avatars.

## Troubleshooting

- No sparkle? Check the style, Completion animations switch, and system reduced-motion setting.
- No sound? The chime is optional, defaults off, and does not play in Calm.
- Opening an old completed task does not replay its celebration.
